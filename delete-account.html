Privacy PolicyDelete your account

You can permanently delete your ThinkRaw account and its data at any time, in the app or by email.

Option 1: In the app (immediate)

  1. Open ThinkRaw and log in.
  2. Tap Profile (the person icon at the top right).
  3. Tap Delete account, then confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away and you are logged out.

Option 2: By email (no app needed)

  1. Email techthinkraw@gmail.com with the subject ThinkRaw account deletion request.
  2. Include the mobile number registered on your account.
  3. To confirm the account is yours, we will send a one-time password (OTP) by SMS to that number or call you on it.

We delete the account within 7 days of confirmation and email you when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your account, mobile number and name
  • Your farm owner, farmer, supplier, buyer or consultant profile, address and pincode
  • Photos you uploaded, ponds and farm records you created, items you listed, and ratings you gave or received

What is kept, and for how long

  • Copies in our backups are overwritten within 30 days.
  • Records we are legally required to keep are kept only for the period the law requires.
  • Farmers added by a farm owner keep their own accounts and records if the farm owner deletes their account.

Deletion cannot be undone. Questions: techthinkraw@gmail.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
