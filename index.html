This Privacy Policy explains how Gopal Singh ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects personal data in the ThinkRaw mobile app ("the app"). ThinkRaw helps fish and agri farm owners manage their farmers and ponds, and connects farmers with suppliers, buyers and consultants near them. This policy is written to meet India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000 and its rules.

1. Data we collect

DataWhen we collect itWhy
Mobile numberWhen you register or log inTo create your account and send a one-time password (OTP) by SMS
Name, business name, address, pincode, GSTIN (optional) and descriptionWhen you complete or edit your profileTo show your profile to relevant users and find people near you
Approximate locationWorked out on our server from the pincode you enterTo show suppliers, buyers, consultants or farmers near you. We do not access your phone's GPS.
Photos you choose to uploadWhen you add a profile, farmer or pond photoTo display them in the app
Farm and pond records (pond size, crops, dates, seed, feed, diesel and medicine use, harvest quantity, selling price, IoT device number)When you or your farm owner enter themTo show your farm records, dashboard and charts
Details of farmers added by a farm owner (name and mobile number)When a farm owner adds a farmerTo link the farmer to that farm and let the farmer log in
Items, prices and stock listed by suppliersWhen a supplier adds itemsTo show them to farmers and farm owners
Ratings and feedbackWhen you rate another userTo show average ratings to other users
Technical logs (IP address, request times, errors)When the app talks to our serverTo keep the service secure, prevent abuse and fix errors

The app has no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, and no in-app payments. We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

2. Who can see your data

  • Other registered users. Your role profile (name, business name, mobile number, address and pincode, description, items you sell and your average rating) is shown to other registered users of the app so they can contact you. If you are a farmer, your name, mobile number and area are shown to registered suppliers, buyers and consultants near you. Farm owners can see the farmers and pond records they manage.
  • Service providers who process data on our behalf, under contract and only for this purpose: Amazon Web Services (servers, database and photo storage in India) and our SMS provider, which receives your mobile number and the OTP so the SMS can be delivered.
  • Authorities, when we are required to share data by law or a valid legal order.

3. How long we keep data

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account, your account, profiles, photos, pond records you created, items and ratings are deleted immediately from our live systems and from backups within 30 days. We keep only records the law requires us to keep, for the period it requires. One-time passwords are deleted as soon as they are used or expire. If a farm owner deletes their account, the farmers they added keep their own accounts and records.

4. Your rights and choices

  • See and correct your profile at any time in the app.
  • Delete your account and data in the app (Profile > Delete account) or from the web: see Delete your ThinkRaw account.
  • Ask for a summary of your data, withdraw consent, raise a grievance or nominate another person to act for you, by writing to techthinkraw@gmail.com.

5. Security

All data between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Login uses one-time passwords with limits on attempts, access tokens expire automatically, and access to production data is limited to authorised staff.

6. Children

ThinkRaw is a business app for adults. It is not meant for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Changes to this policy

When we change this policy we update this page and the "Last updated" date. For significant changes we will also tell you in the app.
